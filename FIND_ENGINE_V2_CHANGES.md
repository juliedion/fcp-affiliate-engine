# Fort Crazypants Find Engine — V2

## Affiliate-first importer
- One-box primary workflow: paste a product/affiliate URL and let the engine research it.
- Merchant/network are auto-detected for Amazon, Walmart, Target, Mavely, Etsy, eBay, plus a generic hostname fallback.
- Pasted URL becomes the affiliate destination by default, so you no longer have to enter it twice.
- Affiliate Find is the default sales mode.
- Retailer/network/destination are summarized in a compact Affiliate Destination card.
- Product source type, direct Shopify sales, manual merchant/network edits, verdict, and compare-at price are moved under Advanced sale options.
- Navigation renamed Product Studio → Find Engine, Saved Products → Saved Finds, Winning Products → Find Ideas.
- Preserves existing AI generation, social, Shopify publishing, product research, image/video, SEO and content tooling.
